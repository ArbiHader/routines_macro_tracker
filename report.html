Net: the Sep-2 breakout attempt failed to hold; BTC is down roughly 3% from that intraday peak but still up about 3% from the Sep-1 close. This move is confounded, not attributable to one cause — the same window saw an Iran-driven gold spike, a sharp dollar reversal, and a blowout jobs report, all discussed below.
